Bay Bedroom Redesign

A calm, modern room built around one idea: the whole entrance wall becomes storage, with a four-door wardrobe and loft cabinets replacing the loose wardrobe and open concrete loft. The bed sits in the half-octagon window bay, under loft cabinets on the old concrete shelf and a flat false ceiling with a recessed LED line. One palette throughout: warm taupe lacquer, walnut and black.

The renders

First the room as you would see it from the door. Then two cutaways from above with the near walls taken away, and four straight views, each looking at one wall with only the wall behind the camera removed, ceiling, fan and LED line in place.

    The storage wall

    The entrance wall is the only one with no window, and it backs onto the hall, not an outside wall, so it stays dry. The wardrobe runs from the corner to the door: four hinged taupe shutters in a walnut surround, crossed by a fluted walnut band, with long black bar handles. Loft cabinets carry on across the full 347 cm, over the door too.

    The other walls

    The bay, the mirror wall and the balcony window seat, drawn flat like the storage wall. The bay's three walls are unfolded side by side so each one can be taped straight off the drawing.

    Layout & clearances

    The bed end is a regular half-octagon, the inside of the octagonal tower: three equal 144 cm walls, lined with plain taupe panels. The bed is centred on the window wall with one floating nightstand on the AC side, and the old concrete shelf carries loft cabinets across the bay. A standing mirror replaces the desk. The balcony wall is built out 33 cm, with a rounded end by the wardrobe, and holds a window seat under the balcony window. A flat false ceiling at 300 cm closes off the hipped roof, with a recessed LED line and the fan.

    Materials

    One palette for the whole room, taken from the wardrobe reference: warm taupe lacquer for the big surfaces, walnut for frames and accents, black for handles and fittings, and warm white everywhere else.

    Warm taupe matt lacquer

    Wardrobe shutters, both lofts, nightstand. PU lacquer on 18 mm BWP ply, which holds up in monsoon humidity.

    Walnut veneer

    Wardrobe surround and fluted band, loft carcasses, open shelves, bed, mirror frame, window-seat top.

    Taupe laminate panels

    The two angled bay walls, up to the loft. Flat panels with thin shadow-gap joints, nothing fancier.

    Black metal

    Long bar handles, curtain tracks, the recessed LED profile and the mirror castors.

    Warm white walls and ceiling

    Matt emulsion. Paint only after the damp repair has dried out.

    Taupe upholstery, linen sheers

    Headboard and window-seat fabric, plus sheers on ceiling tracks in place of the mustard and rust curtains.
