Interior concept · upper-floor bedroom

Bay Bedroom Redesign

A calm, modern room built around one idea: the whole entrance wall becomes storage, with a full-height wardrobe and loft cabinets replacing the loose wardrobe and open concrete loft. The bed sits in the half-octagon window bay under a tall, faceted ceiling. The layout is fixed; below are two finishes to choose from.

Two finishes

Same room, same storage wall, two material stories, rendered with real wood, stone and fabric textures and cloth-draped bedding. Six views each, including the tall faceted ceiling over the bay and the balcony window seat.

    The storage wall

    The entrance wall is the only one with no window, and it backs onto the hall, not an outside wall, so it stays dry. The wardrobe runs from the corner to the door, and loft cabinets carry on across the full 347 cm, over the door too.

    The other walls

    The bay, the desk wall and the balcony window seat, drawn flat like the storage wall. The bay's three walls are unfolded side by side so each one can be taped straight off the drawing.

    Layout & clearances

    The bed end is a regular half-octagon, the inside of the octagonal tower: three equal 144 cm walls. The bed is centred on the window wall, the two angled walls carry fluted panels, the AC-side one with a floating nightstand beside the pillows, and the old concrete shelf becomes a 70 cm-deep oak ledge across the bay. The AC window sits beside the wardrobe, the desk between it and the bay, and the balcony side stays clear as the path from the door. The balcony wall is built out 33 cm, with a rounded end by the wardrobe, and holds a window seat under the balcony window.

    Materials · Option 1

    The Oak & Walnut palette in detail: pale oak for bulk so the wardrobe doesn't close in a small room, dark walnut only where you want depth, and bronze glass as the one reflective note. The other two palettes are listed with their renders under Three finishes.

    Light oak laminate, matt

    Wardrobe, loft shutters, desk. 1 mm laminate on 18 mm BWP ply, which holds up in monsoon humidity.

    Smoked walnut flutes

    On the two bay facets beside the bed, and behind the desk. Pre-finished panels with a 40 mm pitch.

    Bronze glass, black frame

    Centre wardrobe shutter. 5 mm tinted toughened glass in a slim black aluminium profile.

    Warm greige walls

    Matt emulsion on the walls, white ceiling. Paint only after the damp repair has dried out.

    Oak-look SPC floor (optional)

    Click-lock planks laid over the existing tiles, no demolition. Or keep the tiles and let the rug do the work.

    Taupe upholstery, linen sheers

    Headboard and window-seat fabric plus sheers on ceiling tracks, replacing the mustard and rust curtains. Caramel leather pouffe.
